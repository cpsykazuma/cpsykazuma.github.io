Research Interests RESEARCH

  • SAT- and solver-aided cryptanalysis of symmetric-key primitives
  • Differential and linear cryptanalysis and optimal characteristic search
  • Collision and forgery security of hash functions and MACs
  • Security evaluation of lightweight and low-latency cryptography

Selected Research PROJECTS

SAT-Based Cryptanalysis

Solver-aided search for differential and linear characteristics, including divide-and-conquer approaches for reducing difficult search spaces.

ToSC 2025 ↗IEICE 2025 ↗CT-RSA 2023 ↗Code ↗

Hash Functions & MACs

Collision search for AES-like and SPN-based hash functions, and forgery-security analysis of HMAC-SHA and other MAC constructions.

ACISP 2026 (Collisions) ↗ACISP 2026 (LeMac) ↗

Lightweight & Low-Latency Cryptography

Security evaluation of Orthros and other lightweight or low-latency ciphers, and design and security analysis of the microcontroller-oriented tweakable block cipher Rotom.

IEICE 2025 ↗CT-RSA 2023 ↗
